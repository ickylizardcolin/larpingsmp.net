LarpingSMP - recommended mods

Although the server is mostly unmodded, there are a few client-side mods that we strongly recommend are used. All these mods are available on Fabric, and we give examples for it, but are probably on other loaders.

First, you need a good launcher. The vanilla MC launcher sucks for mods. While you do not need to install a community launcher, they make your life much better. Custom clients that allow adding your own mods also work. These are some options:

  • MultiMC
  • PolyMC
  • Prism Launcher

Now, these are the specific mods we strongly reccomend for the clients:

  • AppleSkin - shows extra hunger info
  • Fabric API - depended on by most mods
  • Lithium, Sodium, ImmediatelyFast, FerriteCore - perfomance
  • Mod Menu - configure these mods
  • NoChatReports - if you swear on a private server, you can get banned from all of mc without this mod
  • No Telemetry - stops spying
  • ViaFabricPlus - you can join servers that are newer or older than your client!!!!
